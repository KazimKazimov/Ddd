What is holding alpha back

How it works: the optimizer chooses weights for each country × type × quality bucket to maximize expected alpha against the index, net of trading cost, with cash earning SOFR. Tracking error and beta come from active CTDTS and the covariance of relative spread changes. The bull-case, bear-case and single-name limits use the scenarios, or a standard-deviation spread move where one is missing. Bucket trades are mapped to bonds, and trades below the minimum size are pruned and the problem solved again. DTS is spread duration × spread in bp. Every number here is generated for the mock-up.
